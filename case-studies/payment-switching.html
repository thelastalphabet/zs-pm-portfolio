01Overview

I owned the product strategy and roadmap for a multi-bank payment switching and image-exchange platform that enabled financial institutions to process, clear, route, and settle check payments while automating exception management and operational workflows. I led product development across image exchange, payment clearing, Federal Reserve integrations, and electronic adjustments — partnering with banking customers, operations, engineering, and compliance teams to modernize payment processing and reduce manual effort.

The platform scaled across major financial institutions — including American Express, Quicken Loans, and multiple bankers' bank networks nationwide — by translating complex regulatory requirements into configurable, auditable product capabilities.

02Platform Capabilities

Payment clearing engine

Processed check images, validated transactions, and routed payment files across financial institutions using configurable business rules and complex sorting algorithms.

Electronic Adjustment Management

Designed and launched an enterprise solution allowing banks to initiate, receive, track, research, and resolve payment adjustment cases under FRB and ECCHO operating rules.

Exception & case management

Defined requirements for automated exception handling, case lifecycle management, workflow automation, audit logging, reporting, and document management to streamline investigations and dispute resolution.

Modernized reporting & analytics

Enhanced search, reporting, workflow customization, and operational analytics — significantly reducing manual research and processing effort for bank operations teams.

03Federal Reserve Integrations

Delivered direct integrations with Federal Reserve payment services, including electronic adjustment messaging, inclearing file processing, Documents to Follow (DTFs), accounting extracts, and automated case creation and reconciliation.

Compliance by design: Every workflow was built to translate complex regulatory and operational requirements — Federal Reserve payment standards, ECCHO operating rules — into scalable product capabilities, developed in close collaboration with banking customers, implementation, and operations teams.

04Fraud & Real-Time Verification

Spearheaded the integration of real-time payment verification services (GIACT) into the payment workflow, enabling automated fraud screening, stop-payment detection, configurable business rules, exception routing, and operator review before settlement.
