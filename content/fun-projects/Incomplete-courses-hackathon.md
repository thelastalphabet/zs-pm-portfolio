# Creative Idea that got me the Hackathon 'most feasible' award
An elegant, high-feasibility solution designed to reduce administrative friction and improve the student user experience by optimizing how incomplete courses (ICs) are accessed within the Student portal at WGU. This initiative won the Most Feasible Award for resolving a long-standing operational gap through a simple, high-impact design.

## Executive Summary
Each month, approximately 3,000 incomplete course (IC) requests are approved. Students with an approved IC must finish their course from a previous term while simultaneously navigating their new term.
# The Problem
The current student portal lacks a streamlined mechanism for cross-term course management. Currently, the only way for a student to access an incomplete course from a previous term is to navigate to their Degree Plan page and explicitly click on the previous term.
User Friction: Finding the course requires a minimum of 3 to 4 manual clicks.
Support Overhead: New students unfamiliar with this hidden navigation path frequently escalate issues by calling support, reaching out via social media, or contacting their mentors.
Institutional Cost: These preventable inquiries increase call-center volume, multiply support tickets, and drive up faculty and staff operational costs.
## The Solution: "Past Term" Courses Card Tab
To address this fragmentation without requiring major process changes or extensive re-architecting, the proposed solution introduces a dynamic "Past Term" tab directly onto the existing Courses card on the student Home page.

# Functional Mechanics
Conditional Visibility: The tab becomes visible on the Home page only when a student has an approved, active IC in their current term.
Smart Defaulting: To optimize accessibility, this tab automatically becomes the default active view on the Courses card if an incomplete course exists.
Automated Expiration: The tab is automatically disabled or hidden once the student completes all outstanding ICs or when the designated IC approved period expires (as ICs are only valid for a strict, finite number of days).

## Strategic Impact Analysis
1. High Feasibility
Exceptionally easy to build; all necessary cross-departmental and term data points are already available within the system.
2. Equity & Inclusion
Supports institutional equity by ensuring all students have centralized, frictionless access to their active courses in the exact same manner.
3. User Experience (UX)
Eliminates unnecessary multi-page navigation and contextual backtracking, letting students focus on learning rather than system navigation.
4. Friction Reduction
Removes the immediate need for students to seek outside clarification from mentors, advisors, or social channels to locate their incomplete course.
5. Cost Optimization
Lower support ticket and call volumes directly lower mentor, staff, and overall institutional support costs.
5. Academic Velocity
Improves the overall time-to-complete. Students avoid wasting critical, time-bounded days searching for their materials and can immediately utilize their approved window to finish the course.
