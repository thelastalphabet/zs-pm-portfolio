## My experience
# AI & Automation
I’ve led digital transformations that enable self-service at scale, reducing operational effort while improving the end-user experience. At WGU, I led the company’s first LLM-backed conversational AI product and established organization-wide standards for evaluation, error handling, metrics, and guardrails. That foundation became the starting point for subsequent AI development across the organization.

# Enterprise Strategy
I've led enterprise-wide strategies for conversational AI platform, enterprise communications and portal experiences. My focus is connecting individual product investments to a broader platform strategy so teams can move faster without creating fragmented experiences or duplicated capabilities.

# Edtech Experience
My edtech experience spans PreK-12 and Higher Education. At WGU, I led the transformation of the enrollment experience for the university’s most complex school, combining self-service experiences with workflow automation. The result was an intuitive learner experience that reduced application completion time by more than 75% and saved staff several weeks of manual effort.

I led the development of ACT’s CASEL-aligned Social Emotional Learning product, a comprehensive solution of curriculum and assessments that helps educators assess and improve their students’ social emotional skills. I partnered directly with a team of psychometricians to design and validate assessments for PreK-12 and English Language Learners, including a scoring model validated against a national sample of over 5,000 students, and I introduced quality-control safeguards to protect assessment integrity at scale.  I launched an interactive dashboard feature for SEL assessments that allowed educators and school administrators to easily identify and visualize key SEL data in minutes. The feature increased conversion among existing customers and contributed to new sales.

My broader EdTech experience includes Learning Management Systems such as Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle, and Google Classroom; Student Information Systems such as Banner; and LTI integrations.

# Fintech Experience
I owned the product strategy for a multi-bank payment switching and check clearing platform that processed transactions across Federal Reserve clearing rails for major institutions, including bankers' bank networks nationwide. I designed a configurable validation and cost-control engine, built into the transaction workflow, that screened files against routing rules, fraud signals, and duplicate detection before settlement. I also delivered direct Federal Reserve integrations for electronic adjustment messaging, inclearing file processing, and automated accounting reconciliation. The platform scaled to more than 700 banking institutions and cut processing time by over 75%.

I care deeply about creating clarity in ambiguous environments, developing talent, and building teams that can move with both speed and intention.
