# Social Emotional Learning Product and DLL Sunset


## Slide 1: About Social Emotional Learning Product

- Led the development of ACT’s CASEL-aligned Social Emotional Learning product, a comprehensive solution of curriculum and assessments that helps educators assess and improve their students’ social emotional skills. I launched an interactive dashboard feature for SEL assessments that allowed educators and school administrators to easily identify and visualize key SEL data in minutes. 

# Slide 2: SEL Dashboard: Impact
Addition of a reporting dashboard feature resulted in an increased conversion rate for existing customers including new sales. 


## Slide 3: SEL Dashboard - Why?

- Problem:
- School administrators or educators want to quickly assess their students SEL skills
- Current PDF reports are slow and not interactive
- Our competitors have interactive dashboards
- We failed to bid several RFPs that required interactive reporting.
- If we solved this problem….
- We respond to the VoC - Over 80% are assessment users & remain competitive
- Potential to increase market share by being able to respond to RFPs
- Alignment:
- Aligns with organizations strategic intent to focus on quality and innovative solutions while strengthening its financial health

## Slide 4: SEL Dashboard -  What?

Key Features:
- Hierarchical and role-based access to school admins or educators to view & filter their students SEL skills.
- Filtering capability
- Summary of their top and lowest performing skill
- Quick indicator of students that were flagged or students that are in progress
- Drill down capability based on permission level.

Additional considerations:
- Printing capabilities
- Quick access to documentation
- Ease of  navigation
- Performance

## Slide 5: SEL Dashboard - How? (Rollout)

- Define MVP, and plan future rollouts
- Usability Testing
- Define Requirements
- Release MVP
- Build Prototype


## Slide 6: Dashboard - Challenges & Outcomes

- Challenges:
- Competing with other priorities across organizations’ portfolio that delayed the release of the dashboard
- Technical limitations impacted the amount of filtering we could add.
- Outcomes:
- Increased sales by 10%
- Significant reduction in support thereby reducing costs
- Positive customer feedback on how quickly they could summarize the data and take next steps

## Slide 7: DLL Sunset - Why?

- Problem:
- Not actively selling
- No active development
- Declining revenue and increased costs to maintain product
- Alignment:
- Organization not focused on K-8 market

## Slide 8: DLL Sunset - What?

- Market research to understand competitive landscape
- Internal and external costs & Revenue
- Technology limitations - legacy technology and reliance on external vendor
- Product usage over the years
- Voice of customer
- Outcome:
- Cost of supporting outweighs the Value
- Significant investment to remain competitive
- Not sustainable technology

## Slide 11: Sunset - Challenges & Outcomes

- Challenges:
- Proposed >1- year timeline was not feasible
- Outcomes:
- $600k+ in immediate cost savings
- $80k+ in selling resource metadata to interested customers

## Slide 12: Additional Highlights

- Monthly Reporting Capability

## Slide 13: Additional Highlights

- Created unique market differentiator in a highly competitive market

  - Current contracts (customer & vendor)
  - Infrastructure and technology discussions including infrastructure sunset
  - Determine sunset timeline
  - Understand alternate options to offer to customers.
