## Payment Switching Network
Owned the product strategy and roadmap for a multi-bank payment switching and image exchange platform that enabled financial institutions to process, clear, route, and settle check payments while automating exception management and operational workflows. Led product development across image exchange, payment clearing, Federal Reserve integrations, and electronic adjustments, partnering with banking customers, operations, engineering, and compliance teams to modernize payment processing and reduce manual effort.
# Key accomplishments included:
Led the product vision for an enterprise payment clearing platform that processed check images, validated transactions, and routed payment files across financial institutions using configurable business rules and complex sorting algorithms.
Designed and launched an enterprise Electronic Adjustment Management solution that enabled banks to initiate, receive, track, research, and resolve payment adjustment cases while supporting Federal Reserve (FRB) and ECCHO operating rules.
Defined product requirements for automated exception handling, case lifecycle management, workflow automation, audit logging, reporting, and document management to streamline payment investigations and dispute resolution.
Delivered integrations with Federal Reserve payment services to support electronic adjustment messaging, inclearing file processing, Documents to Follow (DTFs), accounting extracts, and automated case creation and reconciliation.
Led modernization initiatives that enhanced search capabilities, reporting, workflow customization, and operational analytics, significantly reducing manual research and payment processing effort for bank operations teams.
Spearheaded the integration of real-time payment verification services (GIACT) into the payment workflow, enabling automated fraud screening, stop-payment detection, configurable business rules, exception routing, and operator review before settlement.
Collaborated closely with banking customers, engineering, implementation, and operations teams to translate complex regulatory and operational requirements into scalable product capabilities while ensuring compliance with Federal Reserve payment standards.
