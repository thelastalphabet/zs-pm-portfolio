Conversational Insights Agent

A custom GPT that evaluates chatbot responses against conversational-design and UX guidelines — so non-UX experts can judge what "good" actually looks like.

Problem, solution & impact

Problem: A chatbot that ignores conversational design principles erodes trust and adoption — but non-experts struggle to define "good."

Solution: The agent scores responses against UX guidelines, flags gaps, and updates as guidelines evolve.

Impact: Turned vague design critique into actionable, reusable feedback — recommendations can be copied straight into prompt engineering.

Eval Notes Review Agent

A custom GPT that turns messy manual evaluation notes into structured, prioritized themes — open codes, axial codes, and a heat-map summary.

Problem, solution & impact

Problem: Manually reviewing evaluator notes on chatbot responses to spot patterns is slow and doesn't scale past a handful of reviewers.

Solution: Feed it plain text, CSV, or Excel notes — it extracts open codes, groups them into axial codes, and visualizes frequency.

Impact: Processed 64 notes from 4 evaluators in the first week alone, saving ~30 minutes per review and surfacing tone, source-quality, and accuracy as the top issue clusters.

Incomplete Courses, Reimagined

A simple "Past Term" tab concept that collapses a 3–4 click hunt for incomplete-course access into one glance on the student home page.

Problem, solution & impact

Problem: ~3,000 incomplete-course approvals a month, and no direct way to reach that course from the student home page — driving avoidable support calls.

Solution: A conditionally-visible "Past Term" tab on the Courses card — smart-defaulted when an incomplete course exists, auto-hidden once resolved or expired.

Impact: Won WGU's Hackathon "Most Feasible" award for pairing high UX impact with near-zero build cost using data already in the system.
